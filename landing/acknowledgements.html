AI

PhotoCast uses Anthropic's Claude models to help analyse forecast conditions, generate explanations and recommend promising locations.

Forecast analysis uses location names and weather information rather than personal account details.

When you use Ask PhotoCast, your questions and relevant conversation context are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. Please avoid including personal information in your questions.
