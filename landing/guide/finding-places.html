Finding places

Find searches for a place, an area, a region or a day from the forecast screen or the map, with its own keyboard shortcut.

Opening Find

Find is a labelled ⌕ Find button at the top of the forecast screen and in the header of the map. On a computer it reads ⌕ Find a place, region or day with the shortcut beside it.

  • From the keyboard, press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl K elsewhere. It works on the forecast screen and on the map, at any screen width, for every account. Press it again to close Find.
  • From the origin chip. The chip that shows your home or the base you are planning from still opens Find, as well as doing what it always did.
  • / is Ask PhotoCast's key, not Find's.

The shortcut does nothing while a sheet or dialog is open, or while you are typing in a field, so it never takes a keystroke from something else. When it does nothing, your browser gets the keystroke as usual.

What you can search for

Type a place, a region, an area or a day. Results come in groups, in this order: Windows, Regions, Areas, then Places. A window is a day and event, such as a particular sunrise. Before you type anything, Find shows your recent searches.

  • Find ignores your drive-time and rating filters, so a place you name always appears, even if a filter would hide it on the page.
  • A place outside the sub-regions you chose on Lite still appears in results, by name, with no rating.

Areas

PhotoCast knows two kinds of area: named areas such as Hadrian's Wall, and the guidebook sub-regions that cover the coast, hills and dales. Find matches an area on its name and on the other names people use for it, so roman wall finds Hadrian's Wall.

An area's row says what it is and how many of its spots are rated, for example Sub-region · 16 spots · 5 rated, and shows the top rating among them with the sunrise or sunset it belongs to. That figure is only the best of its spots: an area never has a verdict of its own.

  • A named area can sit inside a sub-region, and both can appear for the same search. They are never merged.
  • Lite accounts can search and open areas too. For spots outside your chosen sub-regions the row says how many are outside your picks and shows no rating for them.

Typos

Find forgives one slip in a word of five or more letters: a swapped pair, or a missing, extra or wrong letter. hadrain still finds Hadrian's Wall. An exact match always comes first and is never pushed out by a near miss. Short words such as st and bay have to match exactly, so they do not match half the list.

What a pick does

Choosing a result does what suits where you are: on the forecast screen it opens a sheet, and on the map it moves the map, so a pick never opens something over the wrong layer.

You pickOn the forecast screenOn the map
A placeOpens its next-days sheetFlies the map to it and opens its callout, on the sunrise or sunset you were already looking at
An areaOpens the area sheet: its spots, each with its best window, best firstFlies there and shows only that area's spots, with a Showing … · clear chip to lift it
A regionPlans from that regionPans and frames the map on the region. Your origin does not move
A windowOpens that sunrise or sunset on the mapSelects that sunrise or sunset

On the map, if one of your filters would hide the place you picked, only that filter is relaxed for the moment, and a line says which. Your saved star filter is never overwritten. Find closes any open map menu when it opens, and a pick on the map never opens a sheet or moves your origin.

Each row in the area sheet opens that place's next-days sheet, and Show on map → at the foot of the sheet opens the area on the map.

Your places in FindPro

Pro accounts can bookmark places and areas. When Find opens with nothing typed, a Your places group sits at the top, and a bookmarked result comes first among results that already match what you typed. A bookmarked place never shows up for a search it does not match. More on your places.
