Your placesPro

Star the places and areas you care about, and they come first in Find, in the region lists, on Coming up and in Ask.

Starring a place or an areaPro

A ☆ Your place button sits in the header of the place sheet, in the area sheet, and in the location callout on the Map. Press it and it becomes ★ Your place. Press it again to remove the star. On a phone the button is the star on its own.

  • You can star a single place or a whole area, either a named area or a sub-region.
  • Each change shows a short message, such as Added Steel Rigg to your places., with an Undo button. The message stays for at least ten seconds, and waits while you point at it or focus it.
  • Up to 25. You can keep up to 25 places, counting places and areas together. A twenty-sixth star is refused with You can keep up to 25 places. and nothing changes. Remove one to make room.

Where your places showPro

  • Find. Opening Find shows ★ Your places first, areas before places. A starred result comes first among results that match. See Finding places.
  • Region lists. The Map's ◎ Regions list and the region choices in a sunrise or sunset popup gain ★ My places, which is your starred places plus every place in a starred area, and one entry for each starred area.
  • Marks. A ★ appears beside a starred place's name on the Map, on the spot cards in a sunrise or sunset popup and in the best-spot line on At a Glance. A place that is only in a starred area is not marked on its own.
  • Coming up. A ★ Your places list sits at the top of the page, one row for each starred area and place, with its best window. See Coming up.
  • Ask PhotoCast. Ask breaks an exact tie in your favour. See below.

Stars never change a ratingPro

Starring something never changes a star rating, a verdict or a Best bet, anywhere. The ratings and picks you see are the same ones every reader sees. Your places only decide what goes first when things are otherwise equal, and which places the lists above show.

Ask PhotoCast and your placesPro

When you ask a question that has more than one place at the same rating, such as "Where is best tomorrow?", Ask uses your places only to break that tie. A starred 3★ place never beats an unstarred 4★ one, and a place never beats the forecast's own pick or a better tide fit for being starred.

When a favourite wins on that tie, Ask names both places side by side, so you never lose an equally good option. For example: Mary's Lighthouse is yours, and Bamburgh Castle also scores 4★. If one of your places ranks lower than the best, Ask says so and gives its own rating, for example your Mary's is 4★.

  • Ask How are my places looking this weekend? and it looks only at your starred places and the places in your starred areas. If nothing is starred, it tells you so.
  • Ready questions, the suggested ones Ask prepares in advance, do not use your places. They are the same for everyone.

More on asking in Ask PhotoCast.

On Lite

ProYour places are available with Pro. On Lite you see the ☆ with a Pro tag, and pressing it explains what it adds.

Lite accounts see the ☆ with a Pro tag in the same places, ★ My places with a Pro tag in the region lists, and a one-line Your places teaser on Coming up. Find itself, areas and typo tolerance are not Pro, so Lite accounts have them all.

Private to youPro

Your places belong to your account. Nobody else sees them, and they are kept apart from the sub-regions you chose as a Lite account.
