Guide/Your places

Your placesPro

Bookmark the places and areas you care about, and watch the dates worth waiting for. They come first in Find, in the region lists, in the Your places row at the top of This week and in Ask. Watched dates live in Further ahead and move into This week.

ProYour places are available with Pro. On Lite you will see where they live, with a short explanation.

Bookmarking a place or an areaPro

A Bookmark button sits in the header of the place sheet, in the area sheet, and in the location callout on the map. Press it and it becomes Bookmarked. Press it again to remove the bookmark. On a phone the button is the bookmark mark on its own.

  • You can bookmark a single place or a whole area, either a named area or a sub-region.
  • Each change shows a short message, such as Bookmarked Steel Rigg., with an Undo button. The message stays for at least ten seconds, and waits while you point at it or focus it.
  • Up to 25. You can keep up to 25 places and dates, counting places, areas and watched dates together. A twenty-sixth is refused with You can keep up to 25 places and dates. and nothing changes. Remove one to make room.

Where your places showPro

  • Find. Opening Find shows Your places first, areas before places. A bookmarked result comes first among results that match. See Finding places.
  • Region lists. The map's ◎ Regions list gains My places, which is your bookmarked places plus every place in a bookmarked area, and one entry for each bookmarked area.
  • Marks. A bookmark mark appears beside a bookmarked place's name on the map, in the map's ☰ List and in the best-spot line in This week. A place that is only in a bookmarked area is not marked on its own.
  • The Your places row. A row of one-line chips sits at the top of This week, one for each bookmarked area and bookmarked place (places and areas only), best first. Each chip is the mark, the name, then its best sunrise or sunset and its stars, such as Crimdon Beach · Fri sunrise 4★. A place with nothing worth a trip (nothing above 2★, not rated yet, or outside your picks) comes last and reads quieter. Tap a chip to open the map on that sunrise or sunset. Where a place has nothing worth a trip, the chip opens the place's sheet. Watched dates are not in this row: they live in Further ahead and This week. See Planning a shoot.
  • Ask PhotoCast. Ask breaks an exact tie in your favour. See below.

Watching a datePro

A place or an area is somewhere you care about. A watched date is a day you care about: a king tide, a meteor shower, a solstice. Open the entry in Further ahead and press Watch this date. The button reads Watching while you are, and PhotoCast says Watching King tide · 3 Dec. It's at the top of Further ahead. with an Undo button.

  • Before the date is in the forecast, the entry moves to Watching, a group at the top of Further ahead. It is the same card with the same Watching button, so you can open it, close it and stop watching it exactly as before. It leaves its month while you watch it, and returns to it when you stop.
  • Once it is in the forecast, the date becomes a lane on its day in This week, with the bookmark mark and the plan: the sunrise or sunset, the best place and its stars, and tide on the light where it applies. Tap it to open the map on that sunrise or sunset. If the day already has a lane for the same event, that lane takes the mark and the plan instead of a second lane appearing. A date with no sunrise or sunset of its own, such as a meteor peak, shows clear-sky check on the night and is not a button. If no sunrise or sunset in a tide period has the tide on the light, or a date that is not a meteor peak has no matching topic, the date stays in Watching.
  • No longer listed. If PhotoCast stops listing a date you watch, Watching keeps a quiet row for it, King tide · no longer listed, with a Stop watching button.
  • Stop watching by pressing Watching again. You get an Undo, and the card goes back to its month.
  • Expiry. A date stops showing when the event is over, and PhotoCast deletes the watch 14 days after the date it started, so old dates never use up your 25.
  • The noctilucent cloud season is a stretch of weeks, not a date, so it cannot be watched.

Watched dates, places and areas share the one limit of 25. A watch changes nothing about a rating or a Best bet. More on watching a date.

Bookmarks never change a ratingPro

Bookmarking something never changes a star rating, a verdict or a Best bet, anywhere. The ratings and picks you see are the same ones every reader sees. Your places only decide what goes first when things are otherwise equal, and which places the lists above show.

Ask PhotoCast and your placesPro

When you ask a question that has more than one place at the same rating, such as "Where is best tomorrow?", Ask uses your places only to break that tie. A bookmarked 3★ place never beats an unbookmarked 4★ one, and a place never beats the forecast's own pick or a better tide fit for being bookmarked.

When a favourite wins on that tie, Ask names both places side by side, so you never lose an equally good option. For example: Mary's Lighthouse is yours, and Bamburgh Castle also scores 4★. If one of your places ranks lower than the best, Ask says so and gives its own rating, for example your Mary's is 4★.

  • Ask How are my places looking this weekend? and it looks only at your bookmarked places and the places in your bookmarked areas. If nothing is bookmarked, it tells you so.
  • Ready questions, the suggested ones Ask prepares in advance, do not use your places. They are the same for everyone.

More on asking in Ask PhotoCast.

On Lite

ProYour places and watched dates are available with Pro. On Lite you see the outline bookmark mark with a Pro tag, and pressing it explains what it adds.

Lite accounts see the outline mark with a Pro tag in the same places, My places with a Pro tag in the region lists, a one-line Your places teaser at the top of This week, and the Watch this date button, with a Pro tag, on each Further ahead entry. Find itself, areas and typo tolerance are not Pro, so Lite accounts have them all.

Private to youPro

Your places belong to your account. Nobody else sees them, and they are kept apart from the sub-regions you chose as a Lite account.
