Your placesPro

Star the places and areas you care about, and watch the dates worth waiting for. They come first in Find, in the region lists, in the Your places row at the top of This week and in Ask.

Starring a place or an areaPro

A ☆ Your place button sits in the header of the place sheet, in the area sheet, and in the location callout on the map. Press it and it becomes ★ Your place. Press it again to remove the star. On a phone the button is the star on its own.

  • You can star a single place or a whole area, either a named area or a sub-region.
  • Each change shows a short message, such as Added Steel Rigg to your places., with an Undo button. The message stays for at least ten seconds, and waits while you point at it or focus it.
  • Up to 25. You can keep up to 25 places and dates, counting places, areas and watched dates together. A twenty-sixth is refused with You can keep up to 25 places and dates. and nothing changes. Remove one to make room.

Where your places showPro

  • Find. Opening Find shows ★ Your places first, areas before places. A starred result comes first among results that match. See Finding places.
  • Region lists. The map's ◎ Regions list gains ★ My places, which is your starred places plus every place in a starred area, and one entry for each starred area.
  • Marks. A ★ appears beside a starred place's name on the map, in the map's ☰ List and in the best-spot line in This week. A place that is only in a starred area is not marked on its own.
  • The Your places row. A row of chips sits at the top of This week, one for each watched date, starred area and starred place, with its best sunrise or sunset and its stars. Tap a chip to open the map on that sunrise or sunset. Where a place has nothing worth a trip, the chip opens the place's sheet. See Planning a shoot.
  • Ask PhotoCast. Ask breaks an exact tie in your favour. See below.

Watching a datePro

A place or an area is somewhere you care about. A watched date is a day you care about: a king tide, a meteor shower, a solstice. Open the entry in Further ahead and press Watch this date. The button stays pressed while you are watching, and PhotoCast says Watching King tide · 3 Dec. It joins your places. with an Undo button.

  • Before the date is in the forecast, its chip in the Your places row reads like ★ Geminids · 14 Dec, with in 20 days · best spots once it's forecast underneath. Tap it to scroll to the entry and open it.
  • Once it is in the forecast, the chip shows the plan for that morning or evening: the best place and its stars, and tide on the light where it applies. Tap it to open the map on that sunrise or sunset. A date with no sunrise or sunset of its own, such as a meteor peak, shows clear-sky check on the night instead, which is not a button. If no sunrise or sunset in a tide period has the tide on the light, or a date that is not a meteor peak has no matching topic, the chip keeps its countdown.
  • Stop watching by pressing Watch this date again or the × on the chip, which says Stop watching to a screen reader. You get an Undo either way.
  • Expiry. A chip stops showing when the event is over, and PhotoCast deletes the watch 14 days after the date it started, so old dates never use up your 25.
  • The noctilucent cloud season is a stretch of weeks, not a date, so it cannot be watched.

Watched dates, places and areas share the one limit of 25. A watch changes nothing about a rating or a Best bet. More on watching a date.

Stars never change a ratingPro

Starring something never changes a star rating, a verdict or a Best bet, anywhere. The ratings and picks you see are the same ones every reader sees. Your places only decide what goes first when things are otherwise equal, and which places the lists above show.

Ask PhotoCast and your placesPro

When you ask a question that has more than one place at the same rating, such as "Where is best tomorrow?", Ask uses your places only to break that tie. A starred 3★ place never beats an unstarred 4★ one, and a place never beats the forecast's own pick or a better tide fit for being starred.

When a favourite wins on that tie, Ask names both places side by side, so you never lose an equally good option. For example: Mary's Lighthouse is yours, and Bamburgh Castle also scores 4★. If one of your places ranks lower than the best, Ask says so and gives its own rating, for example your Mary's is 4★.

  • Ask How are my places looking this weekend? and it looks only at your starred places and the places in your starred areas. If nothing is starred, it tells you so.
  • Ready questions, the suggested ones Ask prepares in advance, do not use your places. They are the same for everyone.

More on asking in Ask PhotoCast.

On Lite

ProYour places and watched dates are available with Pro. On Lite you see the ☆ with a Pro tag, and pressing it explains what it adds.

Lite accounts see the ☆ with a Pro tag in the same places, ★ My places with a Pro tag in the region lists, a one-line Your places teaser at the top of This week, and the Watch this date button, with a Pro tag, on each Further ahead entry. Find itself, areas and typo tolerance are not Pro, so Lite accounts have them all.

Private to youPro

Your places belong to your account. Nobody else sees them, and they are kept apart from the sub-regions you chose as a Lite account.
