Guide/Settings

Settings.

Open Settings with the ⚙ button at the top right. On a phone it's in the ⋯ menu, along with Change home postcode and Sign out.

Profile

Your username and email address, with a tag showing whether your account is Lite or Pro.

Home location

Enter a UK postcode and press Look up, or use Use my location on a phone. Check the place name that comes back, then press Save.

Your home sets the sunrise and sunset times shown at the top of the app. On Pro it's also the starting point for drive times and the drive filters. Setting a home works on Lite too.

Local radius Pro

A slider from 10 to 50 miles that sets how far counts as "close to home", for example when you ask Ask PhotoCast for somewhere near home.

Your sub-regions

If your Lite account asks you to choose sub-regions, you'll see your choices here. Lite shows forecast ratings for every location inside the sub-regions you've picked. Locations outside them are marked on the map and in Coming up.

  • You can change your sub-regions freely for a short time after first choosing them. After that, saving a change locks them for 30 days.
  • The status line tells you when you can next change them.
  • If you're under the limit, you can add a sub-region at any time.

ProPro covers every sub-region.

Drive timesPro

PhotoCast works out the drive from your home to every location. After changing your postcode, press Refresh drive times to recalculate. It takes a few seconds and tells you how many locations were updated. The button is only available when your home has changed since the last calculation.

ProPersonalised drive times are available with Pro.

Map colours

Choose the colour scale used on the map and on At a Glance. Your choice is saved to your account, so it applies on every device.

Emails

Tick or untick occasional emails about new features and photography tips. The change saves straight away. Emails about your account, such as password resets, are sent either way.

Deleting your account

At the bottom of Settings you can delete your account. You'll be asked for your password to confirm. Deleting is permanent and signs you out straight away.
