Guide/Settings

Settings.

Open Settings with the ⚙ button at the top right. On a phone it's in the ⋯ menu, along with Change home postcode and Sign out.

Profile

Your username and email address, with a tag showing whether your account is Lite or Pro.

Home location

Enter a UK postcode and press Look up, or use Use my location on a phone. Check the place name that comes back, then press Save.

Your home location determines the local sunrise and sunset times shown at the top of the app. On Pro it's also the starting point for drive times and the drive time filters. Setting a home works on Lite too.

You can change your home location at any time. Changing it clears your drive times until you press Refresh drive times.

Local radius Pro

A slider from 10 to 50 miles (22 by default) that sets how far counts as "close to home", measured in a straight line from your home. It is used only by Ask PhotoCast, for questions about somewhere near home. It is separate from the drive time filter (Any, 45 min, 1h 30 or 2h 30 of road drive time) on At a Glance and the Map.

Your sub-regions

If your Lite account asks you to choose sub-regions, you'll see your choices here. You can choose up to three. Lite shows forecast ratings for every location inside the sub-regions you've picked. Locations outside them are marked on the map and in Coming up.

  • For the first 7 days after you first choose them (counting that day), you can change your sub-regions freely.
  • After that, adding a sub-region while you have fewer than three is always allowed and doesn't start a lock.
  • Swapping or removing a sub-region is allowed once every 30 days.
  • The status line tells you when you can next change them.

ProPro covers every sub-region.

Drive timesPro

PhotoCast works out the drive time from your home to every location. After changing your home, press Refresh drive times to recalculate. The calculation usually takes a few seconds but isn't guaranteed to. When the calculation finishes, it tells you how many locations were updated. There is a 30-minute wait between refreshes.

Map colours

Choose the colour scale used on the map and on At a Glance. Your choice is saved to your account, so it applies on every device.

Emails

Tick or untick occasional emails about new features and photography tips. The change saves straight away. Emails about your account, such as password resets, are sent either way.

Deleting your account

At the bottom of Settings you can delete your account. Deleting your account is permanent. Enter your password to confirm; you will then be signed out.
