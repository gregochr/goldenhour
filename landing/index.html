02 · What it does

A forecast for the shot, not the weather.

/ 01

The map has already made the call

PhotoCast opens on the map with the answer on it: should you go tonight or in the morning, which region is strongest, and where the week's best bet sits. Heat over the ground says poor, middling or worth it before you touch anything.

/ 02

AI reads the atmosphere so you don't have to

AI evaluates cloud cover, humidity, light direction, and even Saharan dust to rate every sunrise and sunset — with dual Fiery Sky and Golden Hour scores Pro plus a plain-English explanation of why.

/ 03

AI picks your best bet — you just show up

AI evaluates every location across the next three days and recommends exactly where and when to go — factoring in day of week and conditions. A heatmap grid shows the full picture at a glance.

/ 04

Catch the dramatic tides at the best light

Spring tides, king tides, and storm surge — flagged and aligned with sunrise and sunset so you're there when the water levels tell a story.

/ 05

Know about aurora before dark, not after

NOAA space weather data, interpreted by AI. When conditions line up for northern lights, you'll get a forecast with enough time to plan your evening.

/ 06

Find the mist before it lifts

AI scores inversion potential for valley and lakeside locations — reading temperature, dew point, wind, and elevation to predict where cloud inversions will create other-worldly scenes.

/ 07

Built for the North

Not a global weather app pretending to know your coast. 200+ handpicked locations across Northumberland, the Lakes, and North Yorkshire.

03 · The story

It started with one question about one sunrise.

One evening last spring, I asked Claude — Anthropic's Artificial Intelligence platform — whether tomorrow's sunrise was worth getting up for. It said yes. So I asked what actually makes a great sunrise: cloud layers, aerosol depth, humidity, light direction.

Then I asked whether a tool existed that combined all of it into a simple go or don't go answer for photographers. Nothing did quite what I had in mind. The best you could do was approximate from cloud cover at the location at the time — nothing about the layers that catch the light, the aerosols that scatter it, or the humidity that kills it.

I decided not to risk the early alarm and the wasted drive. So with 25 years of building production systems for global banks, I started building a better answer. PhotoCast now analyses 10+ atmospheric variables for every sunrise and sunset across 200+ locations — scoring the light, explaining the reasoning, and telling you exactly where to be.

The long summer days are behind us and autumn is on the march. As the landscape photography season returns, we're ready to launch.

05 · Common questions

Questions we hear at 4am.

How accurate is it?

Forecasts are based on third-party weather data and AI interpretation. They're most reliable for tomorrow and the day after. We never claim to predict the sky perfectly — we just give you much better odds than checking a generic weather app.

What areas do you cover?

Northumberland, the Lake District, North Yorkshire Coast, Yorkshire Dales, North York Moors, Tyne and Wear, and Teesdale. Over 200 named locations.

What's the difference between Lite and Pro?

Lite is free and gives you the planner heatmap, weather triage verdicts, and star ratings. Pro adds AI-powered best-bet picks, AI-powered per-location scoring with written explanations, aurora forecasting, and personalised drive times. There are more features in the pipeline.
