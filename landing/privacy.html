Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Your privacy

PhotoCast collects the information needed to run your account, provide forecasts and personalise certain features.

We don't use advertising trackers or sell your personal information.

This policy explains what information we collect, which services help us process it, how long we keep it and the choices available to you.

Who we are

PhotoCast is operated by Chris Gregory, an individual based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy-related queries, contact privacy@photocast.online.

What data we collect

When you register for PhotoCast, we collect:

  • Your email address and username (required for account creation)
  • Your chosen password (stored securely using one-way hashing — we cannot read it)

When you use PhotoCast, we may also collect:

  • Your home postcode and the coordinates it resolves to, if you choose to set one. It sets the sunrise and sunset times shown for your area on every account, and on Pro accounts it also gives drive times from home to the forecast locations
  • Forecast outcome ratings you submit (your rating of how a past sunrise or sunset turned out)
  • Your marketing email setting and when you accepted the Terms. The setting is a choice you make at registration; for accounts an administrator created, and accounts from before the setting existed, it is recorded as on until you change it in Settings. No marketing email is sent today, and none will be sent to an account whose setting was never a choice without asking first
  • If you use Ask PhotoCast, where it is available: the questions you type, how many you have asked each day, and a record of each answered request
  • Usage data: when your account was last active

If you press Use my location, your device’s coordinates are sent once to our server to find the nearest UK postcode. They are not stored in our database: only the postcode you then choose to save is. (If the postcode lookup itself fails, the error our server logs can include the coordinates it sent; see Server logs below.)

Why we collect it

We use your data to:

  • Provide the PhotoCast service (account authentication, personalised forecasts, drive times)
  • Improve forecast accuracy through outcome feedback
  • Send transactional emails (account verification, password resets)
  • Send marketing emails about new features and photography tips (only if you opt in)

Legal basis

We process your data under:

  • Consent — you provide your information when registering and can withdraw at any time
  • Legitimate interest — to operate and improve the service

Cookies and local storage

PhotoCast does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. We use browser local storage to maintain your login session (authentication tokens, your role and username) and to remember your preferences. This is essential for the service to function and is not used for tracking.

Data retention

  • Active accounts: data retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Dormant accounts: a free (Lite) account with no activity in the app for 90 days may be deleted, along with all associated data. We email a warning first, and delete the account no sooner than 14 days after that email, and only if it has stayed inactive. Pro and administrator accounts, and accounts that are disabled or still awaiting verification, are not covered
  • Marketing email preferences: retained until you change them or your account is deleted
  • Ask PhotoCast request log: deleted after 90 days. Where the AI model answered or declined a question, the log keeps the question’s text for those 90 days
  • Server logs: the app’s own log files are kept for 30 days (the same lines are also held in a size-limited container log). They can include your username and email address (at registration, sign-in and whenever we email you), the postcode you save and the coordinates used for your drive times, and, if a postcode lookup fails, the postcode or coordinates it used. Joining the waitlist is logged without your email address. The web server in front of the app keeps a separate access log of each request: your IP address, the time, the path requested (without its query string), the path of the page that made the request and your browser’s user agent. Your sign-in token and the one-time codes in verification and password-reset links are not recorded in it: the app sends your sign-in token in a request header rather than in the address, and the links carry their code after a “#”, which your browser never sends to us. Cloudflare, which carries traffic to and from the app, handles each request in full as part of that service. This log’s retention is set on the host rather than by the app’s configuration and is not bounded by those 30 days
  • Backups: we keep database backups, which contain account data. A deleted account stays in them for up to about five weeks, and in the backup taken before each release until we remove it
  • Waitlist: if registration is full you can join a waitlist (protected by the same Cloudflare Turnstile security check); we store the email address you give, unlinked to any account, until you ask us to remove it (privacy@photocast.online; our administrators can also remove an entry) or the address registers and activates an account. We may email that address an invitation to register: the link works for seven days and only for that address. A registration that is never verified is deleted automatically about seven days after sign-up; we send up to two reminder emails before then
  • Sign-in sessions: an access token lasts 24 hours and a refresh token 30 days; signing out ends all of the account’s current sign-in sessions, on each one’s next request. A password-reset link works for one hour; an email verification link works for 24 hours, and once it has been opened the password must be set within 30 minutes

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in the app: Settings, then Danger zone, then Delete account (you confirm with your password). This removes your account, email address, username, home postcode and drive times, ratings, Ask allowance records and sign-in sessions, and we email you to confirm. Administrator accounts cannot be deleted this way: an administrator’s account is removed by another administrator, or on request to privacy@photocast.online. Some things remain for a time: the Ask PhotoCast request log, whose entries lose their link to you when your account is deleted but are removed only when the 90 days above are up; the app’s server logs (30 days) and the web server’s access log (host-defined retention, see above); and database backups (see above). Copies held by our email provider and Cloudflare are governed by their own policies, and so is anything you typed into Ask PhotoCast, which was sent to Anthropic when you asked: deleting your account does not reach their copy, and their retention is not something we have verified.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — request a copy of what we hold
  • Rectify inaccurate data — edit your home location in Settings, or ask us to correct your email or username
  • Delete your data — delete your account in the app, or request deletion by email (see Deleting your account above for what remains briefly)
  • Data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object to processing — opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • Withdraw consent — you can delete your account at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@photocast.online.

Children

PhotoCast is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has registered, please contact us and we will delete the account.

ICO registration

As a UK-based data controller, we are in the process of registering with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users. The “last updated” date at the top will always reflect the current version.
