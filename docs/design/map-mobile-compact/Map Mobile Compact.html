Give the map the screen

Today the masthead, origin row, tabs, Ask bar and attribution together leave the map under half of the screen. These changes apply below the mobile breakpoint only; desktop and iPad keep the full masthead, including the origin row and sun times.

  1. One-line masthead. The tagline goes. Sun times move up next to the wordmark. Status becomes a dot; the version and Sign out move into the ⋯ menu.
  2. Origin row only when it says something. At home it's hidden, since the HOME marker and rings already show it. Away or unset, it comes back as one line.
  3. Shorter tabs, 13px and 34px tall.
  4. Ask moves into the peek row as its own button. Tapping it opens the sheet with the input focused.
  5. Attribution on one line, truncated, tap to expand.
