Ask PhotoCast

  1. An ask bar sits at the bottom of the map. Tap it for suggested questions or type your own.
  2. The answer is two or three picks, each a place plus a window, with the score, drive time, tide and one line on why.
  3. The map follows the answer. Picks are numbered on the map and everything else dims. Tap a pick to fly to it and set the window pill to that light.
  4. Event questions (“Any rare events coming up?”, “Is there snow on the tops?”) answer from hot topics with event cards, plus spots if they help.
  5. Questions the data can't answer get a plain “Not in the forecast” reply that names what's missing, with related questions to try instead. It never guesses.
  6. Drag the handle down to shrink the answer to one line and see the whole map. Answers come only from PhotoCast's own forecasts.
