Ask PhotoCast · desktop & iPad · proposal

Ask on the big screens

  1. An Ask field sits at the right end of the tab row on every tab. There's room for it there, so nothing goes on top of the map. On desktop, / focuses it.
  2. On desktop the answer docks as a 380 px column on the right. The map or Plan narrows rather than being covered, so you can read the picks and see them at the same time.
  3. On iPad it floats as a card on the right over the map, leaving the window control and the left two-thirds clear. On Plan it pushes the content the same way.
  4. Picks stay linked to the tab. On Map they're numbered and fly to the spot. On Plan the matching window lights up. Plan this on any pick gives leave time, light and tide, and adds it to Coming up.
  5. Same rules as iPhone: suggested questions are Ready (free, answered at the 06:00 run), typed ones use 1 of 3 a day, questions PhotoCast can't answer are caught first, and the Asking about chips show what's sent with the question.
