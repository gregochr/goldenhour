Ask, inside the peek sheet

On Plan and Coming up, Ask is a bar at the bottom of the tab. On the Map, it becomes the top row of the peek sheet that's already there, so no new floating element is added.

  1. The peek grows by one row (74 → 126 px). Window, Tide and Layers stay as they were, underneath.
  2. Tapping Ask expands the sheet and the answer takes the place of the three buttons. Picks are numbered on the map and the rest dim.
  3. Tap a pick to fly to it, or Plan this for leave time, light and tide, and to add it to Coming up. Touching the map shrinks the answer to one line; ✕ clears it and brings the map controls back.
  4. Suggested questions are Ready: answered once per region at the 06:00 run, so they open instantly and are free. Typed questions use one of 3 a day (Pro: 30). Questions PhotoCast can't answer (try “Is the car park busy?”) are caught first and don't use one.
  5. The Asking about chips show what's sent with the question: the window you're on and the map view. Remove the window with ✕.
