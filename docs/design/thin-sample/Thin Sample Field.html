The six decisions

  1. Treatment: hatch around each unrated place. The existing hatch (45°, 7px, 0.6px, bone at .18) is drawn wherever an unrated place's kernel reaches and no rated place's does. The plate now has three states: colour where places were rated, hatch where places exist but were not rated, plain plate where there are no places. A rated place is never dimmed or recoloured.
  2. Population: every place in the field. The hatch counts the whole catalogue the field draws, not the 2h30 reach pool. The legend says so.
  3. Where they meet: colour wins. The hatch is drawn under the field, on the plate, and fades out as rated coverage rises (gone at about one rated place's own peak). Its edge uses the field's own kernel and blur, so it feathers at the same rate as the colour at 300px and at map scale. Line spacing stays fixed in CSS px, so it is the same mark on a card and on the map.
  4. Legend: two swatches. unshaded — not scored becomes a hatched swatch reading not rated yet beside a plain swatch reading no places, after the ramp and haze entries. The right-hand note names the population.
  5. Verdict line: count only when thin. Worth it 4.0★ · 6 rated appears only when the spread row's own gate fails (fewer than 5 rated, or under half the reach pool), so the two lines never disagree. The number is the reach-pool count the verdict was made from. It fits a 300px card on one line.
  6. Map tab: same treatment. Zoomed out, Sat sunrise reads as one warm patch in a hatched region. Zoomed in, the kernel keeps its 7.2 km radius and the hatch keeps its 7px spacing, so the six rated places stay a warm patch with hatched ground around it. Hollow markers show which places are unrated. Where places are sparse, the hatch would break into separate discs with plain ground between them.
Not changed: ramp, bloom, haze, stars, verdict words, and the whole-plate hatch for a window with no ratings.
