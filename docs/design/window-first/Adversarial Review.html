Before the pilot — what I would actually do

Three cuts, one afternoon, then ship it to friends

A pilot with friends is the impartial review — better than anything on this page, because it is real people at 05:00. Do not rebuild for it. Make the three cuts that reduce explaining, leave everything contested in place, and let the pilot tell you what to remove next.

  1. Rename reach into plain words — "How far tonight · 2h30" — and delete the explainer link. Charge 05.
  2. One source for reach — lens states it, rail stops repeating it. Charge 06.
  3. Desktop-only arrows, and drop the exposure counter from signal decay so the app is reproducible while five people are reporting bugs. Charges 02 and 07.

Leave alone for the pilot: the eight-layer night, the tide rows, Coming up, Also Good. All four are cheap to remove later and impossible to evaluate without users. Strip the five demo buttons before anyone sees it.

The counter-proposal to test against

The four-element Plan

Not a serious proposal — a control. Build it in an hour, run it beside the current one for a week, and the difference tells you which of the middle layers are real. If this version answers "go or not" as fast as the rich one, everything the rich one adds is for the second question, not the first — and second-question material can live behind a tap.

  1. Rail — four days, verdict and reach each.
  2. One window card per shooting window — time, verdict, best rating, count.
  3. Four spot cards — name, region, drive.
  4. One line of why — the Best Bet sentence, nothing else.
