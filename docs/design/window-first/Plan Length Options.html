Option 1

Window-first agenda — same three tabs

The outer loop is time, not feature. Each shooting window is one card that carries everything relevant to it: its verdict, its Best Bet, its nearby spots, and its tide / NLC / aurora alignment as badges rather than as sections further down the page. Tides and aurora stop being blocks that repeat per run — they become attributes of the window they affect. Regions, Almanac and the Full Briefing collapse to three doors at the bottom.

Option 3
