Map tab · iPhone · proposal

Map first, panels on request

Nothing opens on load. Two ways to fold the window picker, tide and layer controls away, both live below. Step the window with ‹ › or pan inland to see the tide control come and go.

  1. Closed by default. The window pill stays at the top; everything else starts as a one-line summary.
  2. One panel at a time. Opening one closes the other. Touching the map closes whatever is open.
  3. Tide shows up only when it matters: the selected window is Maybe or Worth it, and at least one coastal spot is in view. Filters › Tide lets you set it to Always or Off.
  4. The summary carries the tide reading (“Mid ↓ · 21 dim”), so most of the time you don't need to open the chart.
  5. The “scored locations shown” toast fades after 3 s.

Option A

Peek sheet

Window, Tide and Layers sit in one bottom sheet as three summary buttons. Tap one to open the sheet on that section; tap it again or touch the map to close. Everything is in one place, one thumb-reach away.

Option B

Chips on the map

The pill's ▾ opens the window list. A tide chip sits above the control bar and opens a compact chart card. Closer to today's layout with less on screen; the controls are spread across the screen rather than in one place.
