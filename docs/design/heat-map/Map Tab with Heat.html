One field, two surfaces — the Map tab on the shared engine

This tab and the Plan tab now draw from one location catalogue and one field kernel, so a colour cannot mean two things in two places. The kernel is host-agnostic: the Plan thumbnails project with real coastline geometry, this map projects through the tiles, and both share the colour ramp, the coverage clamp, the confidence haze and the cull that keeps a growing catalogue from stalling pan and zoom. It also opens on your planning area — everything under 3h — rather than zooming out to fit Skye, because opening on the whole of Britain is not a glance. The whole catalogue is one tap away.

What consolidating bought

One kernel, two hostsThe field computation takes screen-space points and knows nothing about maps. The Plan tab feeds it a d3 projection clipped to real coastline; this tab feeds it the tile map's own projection. Fixing the field once fixes it everywhere — which was not true an hour ago, when a performance fix landed on one surface and not the other.
One catalogueBoth tabs read the same locations, so adding Scotland and the Peak District appeared here without touching this file. Previously this view carried its own copy and would have silently missed new coverage.
The cull is what makes it scaleA location that cannot reach a single cell no longer costs an iteration per cell. Without it, 204 locations stalled the render outright; the honest next test is a thousand.

The division of labour

This tab is space; Plan is timePlan asks which of my six windows and shows space inside each. This asks what about here — pan to a headland, a valley, a road you are driving. Same field, different question, which is what stops two maps cannibalising each other.
Heat is the default nowMedallions stay one tap away as the honest comparison, and at anything wider than a county they show why: a cluster of forty averages its own gems away. That was always true; it only became visible once coverage grew.
Opening on your area, not your catalogueFraming to all the data would open on the whole of Britain and give you three green pixels. The same three-hour rule the Plan tab uses decides the opening view, and Whole catalogue is there when you want it — with a note naming what sits beyond.
