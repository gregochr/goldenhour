## Outside your sub-regions: the field, the dots and the legend (sub-regions S5a-1, 2026-10-27)

Drawn **only for a body the server shaped** (`photocast.sub-regions.enforce` on and a reader who is not positively Pro or Admin). Shaped means `Array.isArray(allowedLocationIds)` on the briefing, which `WindowFirstBriefingContext` publishes (an empty array is shaped with zero picks); the pane puts it on `heat.shaped` and `heat.allowedCount`. Never the role, never a client test of an id against the list. Pro, Admin and flag-off draw byte-identical to before (the layer passes no key, and tests assert the kernel sees no `outside`).

**A place outside the picks is its own heat state.** `heatSpots.windowState` returns `'gated'` from the score row's `gated: true` (also from a forecast row, for a past window), ahead of the sky-subject test, so a gated wood is gated too. It is in neither the colour pool, nor the "not rated yet" hatch, nor `thinRatedCount` (rated or total), nor the Map's `scopeCount` and the counts footer's "x of y shown". It is never "not rated", "0" or "—". The window's served `bestRating` is stripped for a shaped Lite response, so `unratedPoints` and `windowUnscored` ask the place states (`pickScope.hasRatedState`) whether any of the reader's own places is rated instead; a reader with no picks gets no "not scored yet" line. A wood or hide with no score row at all cannot be told apart from an allowed one and counts as allowed for the veil and the outline (the briefing's canopy slots are gated, but the field reads score rows only).

**The field (`utils/heatField.js`, drawn by `MapHeatLayer`).** `drawTiles` (and `drawGeo`, for the Plan's S5b) take `opts.outside` (the gated places) and `opts.allowed` (every other place, rated or not):

- **Veil**, under the colour and the hatches, on the plate: a flat wash where an outside place's kernel (radius x 1.15) reaches and no allowed place's does; it fades to nothing as allowed coverage reaches 0.5. Same blur + 1px, same land clip and opacity as the hatches (`paintMask`, split out of `paintMasked`). Ink `rgba(156,158,160,.18)` (token `--color-outside-veil`): the handoff's `rgba(150,160,170,.20)` sat within a few units of `--color-topic-air` (#8FA3B8), whose rings can be up at the same time, so it was taken to a neutral grey at a lower alpha.
- **Outline**, drawn last: a dashed bone line (`rgba(242,231,211,.85)`, dash 5/3, 1.4px zoomed out to 1.6px zoomed in, `outlineWidthAt`) at the 0.42 contour of allowed coverage (radius x 0.9), clipped to land. The contour is a hand-written marching squares (`contourLines`), joined into polylines because a canvas dash pattern restarts at every sub-path. One allowed-coverage grid (radius x 0.9) serves both the outline and the veil's suppressor (the handoff suppresses at radius x 1.0; sharing the grid was asked for). The grids are sampled at `max(grid, min(radius / 4, 12))` px, because planning is redone on every frame of a pan: on a 260-place roster that is about 7 to 13 ms at radii of 30 to 90 px (it was 12 to 42 ms at the field's own pitch).
- Both honour `opacity` (the handover fade and Ask's dim) and `focus`, and are passed only below `LAND_CLIP_MAX_ZOOM` with the land path in hand. They apply on astro nights too (the conditions rows carry `gated`); aurora is Pro-only and is never gated. With zero picks the veil covers the land and there is no outline.

**Markers.** A gated place keeps its name chip, which is the tap target and opens the location sheet as ever; its marker is a small solid grey dot (`.wf-maplab-chip-m[data-gated]`, `--color-outside-dot`), it shows no star, its accessible name is "<name>, outside your sub-regions" and it never takes the outcome-rating clause. The star floor does not hide it (the rating filter lets a gated place through; type, drive, dark-sky, scope and the lens still narrow it).

**The pill.** The shaper strips every region `meanRating`, which `pickTopEligibleRegion` ranks by and skips without, so `mapVerdict.windowVerdict` takes the served `shapedLeadRegion` (`buildShapedLeadIndex`) when the body is shaped, falling back to the same order (verdict-eligible, tier, name) over the in-scope records when the lead lies outside the scope. A region that scored nothing states no verdict, as an unshaped one without a mean does not. No star is shown for a shaped reader.

**Legend (`MapLegendPanel`, `picks` prop).** After "not rated yet / no places": a grey swatch row "outside your sub-regions" carrying a plain `wf-lens-pro` **Pro** pill (a label, not a door), and a dashed swatch row "your sub-regions" (omitted with zero picks, when no outline is drawn). Note: "Colour shows the {n} place(s) in your sub-regions. Pro rates all {total}." ({n} the served allowed count, singular for one; {total} the roster), or with zero picks "Choose your sub-regions in Settings to see ratings." (text only; the button comes in S5a-3). Not shown on an aurora night.
