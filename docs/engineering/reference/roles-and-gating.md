## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| `ADMIN` | All endpoints + Operations tab |
| `PRO_USER` | Forecast, past-window ratings, locations, re-runs |
| `LITE_USER` | Read-only forecast; sees the Map's rating block locked (`PUT /api/outcome/ratings` is PRO/ADMIN) |

> The legacy `/api/outcome` stack (`GET /api/outcome`, `GET /api/outcome/all`, `POST /api/outcome`) was deleted in code (audit-followups O1): it had no UI since 2026-02-27 and `actual_outcome` (dropped in V195) had no user link. Readers contribute observations through `PUT /api/outcome/ratings`.
>
> ⚠️ **The Map tab's per-reader rating is the other way round, on purpose.** `PUT /api/outcome/ratings` (V177 `outcome_rating`, the
> "How was it?" block) is `@PreAuthorize` **PRO_USER and ADMIN**: a LITE write is a 403 whatever the client does, and the client shows
> LITE the block locked (see *Role gating — UI pattern*). The old `POST /api/outcome` calibration
> input no longer exists, so the ratings are the gate's only input (*Calibration gate*); a LITE account's own `GET /api/outcome/ratings` answers `[]`
> (no role gate on the read). `UserRole`'s javadoc no longer calls outcome recording a LITE right in general. The optional `seen` tag on that PUT (V187) rides the same gate: a LITE tag is a 403.

> **Ask PhotoCast is Pro and Admin only** (2026-10-24 owner decision, `ask-pro-gating-plan.md`). LITE is 403 `NOT_ENTITLED` on `POST /api/ask` and `GET /api/ask/ready`, flag on or off, from a `SecurityConfig` matcher in the security chain (backed by a class-level `@PreAuthorize` and a guard at the top of `AskService.admit` and `ask`); `GET /api/user/settings/ask` stays 200 for every role, so the client can still tell the flag being on from it being off, and the client derives the lock from the role, as the drive filter does, with no `entitled` field. There is no LITE allowance (`limit-lite` is removed). History: until 2026-10-24 Ask was open to every role with a daily allowance (LITE 3, PRO and ADMIN 30).
> The aurora rule below is kept because `AskUserContext.canSeeAurora()` still serves a user-less Ready conversation: an aurora RATING (from `aurora_forecast_result` or `AuroraStateCache`, which `GET /api/aurora/forecast/results` serves to PRO_USER and ADMIN only) is withheld from anyone who is not ADMIN or PRO_USER, and from a user-less conversation.

> **Account deletion is open to every role except ADMIN, and the postcode prompt is for every role** (login-registration series,
> 2026-10-17). `DELETE /api/user/account` is `isAuthenticated()` with a password re-check, so a LITE reader deletes their own
> account as freely as a PRO one; an ADMIN's attempt is a 409 (another admin deletes an admin through `DELETE /api/users/{id}`).
> `postcodePromptDue` has no role term, so the sign-in postcode prompt and the registration `HOME` step reach LITE, PRO and
> ADMIN alike — saving a home postcode has always been open to every account, and the Pro line beside it ("Also gives you drive
> times from home") is text shown to every role. The server gates drive times by role (D2, `pro-features-gate-plan.md`):
> `POST /drive-times/refresh`, `GET /drive-times` and `GET /reach` are 403 for LITE, and `DriveTimeRefreshJob` (nightly and the admin
> "Run now") measures only enabled PRO_USER and ADMIN accounts with a home. Saving a home and `GET /api/user/settings` stay open to every role.

## Product Strategy

See `docs/product/` for detailed reference documents.

**Differentiators**: Claude-generated "why" explanation | AOD+PM2.5 aerosol proxy | Cloud approach risk detection (temporal trend + upwind sampling) | Location type-specific UI | Outcome recording feedback loop

**Freemium split** (rewritten 2026-10-27 by sub-regions S6, superseding the 2026-10-25 split; the public pricing list, FAQ and Terms §4 say the same): **LITE** gets the star rating, the verdict, stand-downs, region prose, hot topics and Coming up, with per-location ratings **only in up to {limit} sub-regions the reader chooses** ({limit} is the admin-set `sub_region_policy` row, default 3). A reader's first choice starts a free week (changes are free until `today + grace-days - 1`, default 7 days); after that, adds under the limit are free and removing a pick locks further removals for 30 days (`lock-days`). Ratings outside the picks are stripped and marked `gated` at the controller edge (`LitePickShaper`; enforced in production by `photocast.sub-regions.enforce: true`), never on the client; see `sub-regions-plan.md`. **PRO and ADMIN** see every sub-region, and add the Fiery Sky and Golden Hour scores, the per-location written explanation, the best-bet picks, drive times from home, the tide curve and match, Ask PhotoCast, past-window rating and the aurora forecasts (status, per-location ratings, the field, the viewline and the stored run's results). The aurora hot topic stays free for every role (the two-question rule), and LITE sees the alert level in the aurora banner (`LiteAuroraBanner`, from the role-free `GET /api/aurora/alert`, with a Pro door where Pro's field, best site and viewline are; `aurora-lite-alert-plan.md`), plus the Pro door under the Map's window list (`aurora-pro-door-plan.md`; see `aurora.md`). Both run to the same horizon. There are no blurred metrics: a Pro field is absent from LITE's payload, never sent and obscured. The scores, the written explanation, best bets and drive times are being gated by `pro-features-gate-plan.md` (S1–D2); until each phase merges, that feature still reaches LITE. Drive times from home are server-enforced since D2 (three endpoints 403, job skips LITE); stored LITE rows are left in place.

### Role gating — UI pattern

Every new UI feature must be assessed for role gating.

- **Admin-only** (Operations tab, health widget): hidden entirely
- **PRO/ADMIN** (all other premium features): visible to LITE users, with a "Pro" pill. A Pro feature's locked surface **opens a worded upsell** (owner Q13, 2026-10-24: a live trigger with the pill inside it, a non-modal popover or inline section, no link, no "upgrade" text). **Named exception (Coming up's locked tide-light row, owner Q3, 2026-10-31): a static inline row with no trigger, because its sentence IS the worded upsell and a trigger would only open a popover repeating it** (`coming-up-tide-light-locked-row-plan.md`); the tide teaser and Ask PhotoCast do
  - The greyed lock (`opacity: 0.45`, `pointer-events: none`, `disabled` on interactive elements) is only for controls too small to carry an upsell
  - Upsell text: "Pro" pill badge (inline) or "Upgrade to Pro" (standalone) — ⚠️ text only, never a link: `/upgrade` is not a route and the tests that pin it (`UserSettingsModal.test.jsx`, the since-deleted `MarkerPopupContent.test.jsx`) give the App Store's rule against pointing at a web purchase as the reason; the auth screens' `ProTag` is text in a `<span>`
  - The sub-regions doors (sub-regions S5a-3, drawn only for a Lite reader whose briefing body the server shaped by their picks): **Ratings across the {region}** in a region panel with none of their picks, **Every sub-region** under a region panel with picks, and **Ratings at {place}** in the location sheet of a place outside their picks (`SUBREGIONS_ALL_`, the `subregionsRegion*` and `subregionsPlace*` copy in `shared/ProUpsell.jsx`; `SUBREGIONS_COAST_` and `SUBREGIONS_DARKSKY_` are Coming up's, S5b-2: **Coastal spots everywhere** / **Dark-sky spots everywhere**, one door on the first Coming up entry showing the "none of your sub-regions" line, a sibling after the card, skipping any entry that carries the Lite locked tide row (`lightRowsWithheld`), and falling back to the first when every one does; a reader with no picks gets the Choose button only). Each is `ProUpsellDoor` unchanged (an optional `note` line aside), one per surface, counts served, singular at one; the status buttons beside them are S4's (Choose, Change, Add); see `map-tab.md`.
  - The Map's bottom-left viewline chip is one such door ("Viewline", opens upward) shown to a LITE reader during a real aurora alert; see `map-tab.md`.
- **Exception**: elements too small for visual disable stay hidden; upsell goes on the nearest tappable surface (the popup)
- Backend 403 gating is the real enforcement; frontend greying is the UX layer
