**Plan tab (the day × event MATRIX)**: The Plan tab — no flag, no default, no frozen comparison
control (the earlier "v1" layout and its `usePlanLayout` toggle were retired in full,
`docs/engineering/v1-retirement-plan.md`). The pane is
**six pictures**, not a list: `WindowFirstHeatStrip` draws a day-column × sunrise/sunset-row grid of
heat-field thumbnails (`utils/windowFirstMatrix.js` for the grid maths, `utils/heatField.js` for the
kernel), each card carrying the served verdict word and tint — and, beside the word, the served region MEAN in quiet ink, always one decimal (`3.5★`, `verdictMean`, 2026-10-13 H1), printed beside a verdict-eligible word and beside `UNRATED` ("Too few rated 3.6★" — the figure with the qualifier is the honest card), silent beside an ineligible region's triage `STAND_DOWN` (a figure beside "Poor" would contradict it) and beside AWAITING, so a 3.5 and a 4.6 no longer wear one word alike. Beside a BLANKED stand-down (the word's own region has empty served `slots`, `verdictBlanked`) the card prints `unsettled` in the same quiet ink and voices "Poor, too unsettled to forecast" (2026-10-21 H4; the popup head mirrors it), so "Poor" over a hatched field is never read as a rating. The card also carries a star-band spread histogram, the
best-reachable line (which, when the reach-gated pool holds no rated spot but the window's word rests on ratings, reads `Region <name> · beyond <tier>` instead of "not scored yet" — H2, only with a measured drive and an active tier, the only way that state arises); ⚠️ it names the first reach-pool spot that is not a served `neighbourOutlier`, `bestReachOf`; if an outlier was skipped and the next rated spot is under the named floor 3 (`NEIGHBOUR_NAMED_FLOOR`) or none is rated, nothing is named and the card carries `bestReachWithheld`, drawn as muted "no clear pick" and spoken with its reason, tested BEFORE the "not scored yet"/beyond-reach fall-throughs; the title and spoken reason say "within reach" only with a measured drive; the popup header and the search figure take the same state, the dawn-race fallback skips an outlier, and the page-level floor message's `ceilingOf` keeps the figure but names no outlier; the pool, histogram and counts still hold the outlier, and with none flagged the pick is unchanged; its topics and the served `BEST BET`/`ALSO GOOD` legend; empty cells say why
(*this morning has gone* / *past the end of the forecast*) and a travel day is a div, never a button. While an Ask pick is chosen its card also wears the pick (`highlightKeys`, a `Map` of `date:targetType` → rank, → `data-ask-highlight`: the spec's gold border plus the pick's rank circle in the card's top row beside the sun word, "Ask pick N" last in the accessible name, scrolled into view) — deliberately a different mark from `data-open`, which is almost the same gold; it never opens the popup, is live only with the Plan tab in force, Ask's sheet not covering it and Ask available, and an away cell highlights nothing.
⚠️ **The field hatches the ground around places nobody has rated** (2026-10-22, `docs/engineering/thin-sample-plan.md` F1/F2/F3, owner decisions Q1/Q2): `buildHeatSpots` carries a per-window state beside each score — `rated`, `stood` (a null rating with a `triageReason`: looked at and stood down), `unrated` (a SKY place with neither, a missing row included) and `na` (a wood or wildlife hide, never rated by design) — and `unratedPointsFor` returns the `unrated` places only, so a wood or hide stays PLAIN ground and is never in this hatch (a weather stand-down has its own, below). The thumbnails and the open row's field map pass them to `drawGeo` (`heatField.js`'s unknown-ground mask: 4px grid, the field's own radius and bucket walk, drawn above the plate and under the colour, so colour wins where a rated place is near; a focused region fades its hatch with its colour). ⚠️ **Three gates, all deliberate:** (1) no hatch while the scores response has not loaded successfully (`scoresLoaded`, set on SUCCESS only in `WindowFirstBriefingContext` — a failed or in-flight fetch is not evidence, and served windows on Plan and Map both wait for it); (2) no per-place hatch on a window with no rating (the whole plate is already hatched, decided by `card.bestRating`, never an empty point set) or on an away cell; (3) a window with no `rated` and no `stood` place at all yields no hatch (no evidence either way, `unratedPointsFor`). Solar windows only: `AuroraFieldThumb` is untouched. The legend under the strip now always draws two swatches, `not rated yet` (hatch) and `no places` (plain plate), in place of the conditional `unshaded — not scored` note; the old desktop-only right-hand explainer line ("Colour and hatch cover every place in the forecast …", `.wf-hstrip-sp`) was removed on 2026-10-25, so the swatches stand alone at every width. The row-map's region focus now also lands on a region whose places are ALL unrated. **The verdict line's `· N rated`** (F3, `utils/windowFirstThinSample.js` `thinVerdictCount`): the Spread row's own `rated` over the card's REACH pool (one figure with `6/83 rated`), shown only for `WORTH_IT`/`MAYBE` (an allow-list), when `hasSpreadSample` fails and at least one place is rated; never beside `UNRATED`, `STAND_DOWN`, `AWAITING`, an away cell or an unscored window. It is drawn only where the card's container is at least 190px wide (`@container wf-card`, `.wf-hc-thin`; the verdict row must not wrap and push the Spread and Best rows out of line), so on the narrowest phone cards it is absent (accepted: the hatch and the Spread row's `N/M rated` carry it), and it is NOT voiced separately — the spread clause ("6 of 83 rated") already speaks it. The Map tab's twin is in the Map tab (v2) bullet.

The count is `{n} spot(s)` or `{n} of {m}` over the reader's places with `· {m} outside` apart; the lens bar's `spotCount` and the rail's `drawnCount` count the reader's places only. The field map names the reader's places only (chips).
- **Silence.** The shaper strips every region mean, so `regionSeries` holds nulls and the popup's "region's own best window" line draws nothing; no surface says "not scored" for it.
- **Accepted residual.** An allowed spot's `neighbourOutlier` is computed server-side against every neighbour, outside places included, so a reader's place can be skipped by the "best reachable" naming because of a neighbour they cannot see (the star stays; the Best row may say "no clear pick").
