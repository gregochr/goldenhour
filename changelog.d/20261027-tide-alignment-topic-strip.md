### Added — the tide rows on the Map's four-day strip now show where the water meets the light

On the Map tab's "Hot topics · next four days" strip, the Spring tide, King tide and Storm surge rows carry an `HW` (filled) or `LW` (outlined) mark over every sunrise or sunset that has that water within 90 minutes of the light, and nothing over a mid-tide window. The window on the map has a ring on its mark and a line under the grid that says what it is: `Fri sunrise 06:56 · high water 08:05, 1h09 after · 4.6 m · North Shields`, or, for a window the water misses, `mid tide · high water 18:57, 2h17 after`. The topic chip above the map gains the same verdict between its name and its count (`HW on the light`, `LW on the light`, `mid tide`). A tide run's label gains a second line with its peak day and range (`peak Tue · 4.9 m`; just `peak Tue` on the phone sheet), and a run that reaches the last window shown ends in a dashed edge. A bar's accessible name now lists which windows have high or low water on the light.

All of it is Pro and Admin only: a Lite reader's rows, chip and locked tide doors are unchanged. The 90-minute call is the server's. `BriefingWindowTide` (on `GET /api/briefing`) gains `lightAlignment` (`HW`, `LW`, or `NONE`), the nearest extreme's `nearestHeight` and the station's own `eventTime`, all stripped for Lite in `forLite()` with `nearestOffset`. Big seas, whose HW ticks are a different event, is untouched.
