### Changed — T+1 evaluation cache cap drops from 8 h to 6 h (solar refresh P3 prep)

`photocast.freshness.horizon.t1-hours` now defaults to 6 (was 8). With sun-relative cycles the
shortest gap between two cycles is the midwinter morning-to-afternoon gap (~7 h 24 min at Durham,
less the morning batch's latency), which an 8 h cap would exceed, so the afternoon cycle would skip
a T+1 slot as cached. 6 h keeps about an hour's margin all year. The cap is applied to both cycles
by the shared CACHED gate in `BriefingCandidateCollector`, before eligibility.

Behaviour-changing today as well, but only in principle: under the current 01:00 / 14:00 schedule
the gaps between cycles are 13 h and 11 h, so a T+1 entry written by one cycle is always older than
8 h (hence also 6 h) at the next, and the cap cannot bite between scheduled cycles. It can only bite
for a manual or restart-recovery run within 6-8 h of the previous cycle, which now re-evaluates
instead of reading the cache. A hand-edited `t1-hours` override on the production host is not
changed by this; remove or lower it there before switching the afternoon job to SOLAR_OFFSET.

Text-only: rationale in `IntradayEligibilityPolicy`, `AstroConditionsRefreshJob`,
`PipelineOrchestrator` and `application-example.yml` no longer hard-codes 01:00 / 14:00; the
stability-snapshot log line no longer labels a >12 h age as "recovered after restart" (log only).
