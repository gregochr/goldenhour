### Changed — A switched-off region can no longer hold switched-on locations

Turning a region off is now refused while any of its locations is switched on ("Turn off its 3 locations first."), and switching a location on, adding one, or moving a switched-on one into a switched-off region is refused ("Turn Northumberland on first."). A failure reset in a switched-off region clears the counters but leaves the place off. Existing half-off data is left alone. The Regions and Locations lists now show the server's sentence when a toggle is refused.
