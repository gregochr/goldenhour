### Changed — the Operations tab's sub-tabs are grouped by job

The Operations tab's two groups (Data and Operations) are now five, named for the job each sub-tab does: Runs (Pipeline Runs, Job Runs, Scheduler, Travel Days), Tuning (Run Config, Sky Eval), Places (Locations, Regions, Tides), People (Users, Waitlist, Sub-regions) and Testing (Hot Topics, Rewind). The tab opens on Runs / Pipeline Runs, and the waitlist count now also shows on the People button so it stays visible from the landing group. Every existing `#manage/<tab>` link still resolves, because tab values did not change.
