### Changed — every Plan-tab map door opens the Map tab instead of the overlay (overlay convergence, C4)

Fourth phase of retiring the Plan tab's map overlay (`docs/engineering/overlay-convergence-plan.md`, O-6). The seven producers that opened the "Aurora tonight" / "Sunrise tomorrow" modal now all take the ONE structured `source:'plan'` door the location sheet footer already used, and the overlay can no longer be opened:

- **The Regional planner's drill-down pin** (an `event` trigger), **its location names** and **the spot sheet's rows** (`location`), and **the pick dialog's *Show {region} on map* / *Open {location} on map*** (`region` / `location`) go through the shell's `openMapTab`. It closes the pick dialog, spot sheet and window popup FIRST, merges the Plan's live rating floor and reach tier (`limitMinutes`), and `App.openMapTabFromPlan` resolves the window, region, location, spot-set lens and headline. A region door still lands on that region's top-rated spot, headed by the region's name, exactly as the overlay framed it.
- **Coming up's coastal and dark-sky cards and their day chips** send the `coming-up` trigger: the exact spot set becomes the breadcrumb's named, clearable lens (`focusNames`) with its subject filter, and the headline names the card and day.
- **The aurora banner** (shown on every tab) sends an `aurora` trigger for the night in progress. It reads the Plan's lens from the briefing context it already sits in, so a banner door imports the reader's rating floor and reach tier like a card does. Pressed on the Map tab itself it is a window change only (`source:'map'`, no lens, scope or camera write, no tab request); `MapView` now follows a night named by a map-sourced handoff.
- **A SIMULATED alert has no map** (C3 / aurora-banner-plan D9), so its banner is honest instead of dead: an admin's press opens the Operations tab ("Generate scores →"); for anyone else the banner is a statement with no tab stop and the call to action reads "Test alert". A real alert with no Map tab to reach (no forecast, its night over) shows no call to action either, and the old hash writes (`#map`, `#manage`, both no-ops since v1 retirement D1) are gone. LITE accounts still receive no aurora status, hence no banner and no door.

`App` loses `handleShowOnMap`, `openFullMapTab`, the `mapOverlay` state, the settings-edge overlay close and the `MapOverlay` mount; `WindowFirstShell` loses its `onShowOnMap` prop. A door no longer writes any state the Plan tab reads. The v1-retirement §8 item 9 route (an overlay opened OVER a Plan dialog by the planner's 🗺 or the banner) is closed rather than guarded. `MapOverlay.jsx`, `utils/mapOverlay.js` and `MapView`'s `overlayMode` branches are untouched until C5.

Known limits: the aurora `detail` prose and astro explanations the overlay's popup showed still have no home (plan §3 C2 note, an open owner call); a door pressed while the Map tab is in force with a standing door lens clears that lens (a map-sourced handoff ends it, as it does for the sheet footer).
