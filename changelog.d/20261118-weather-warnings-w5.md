### Added — Weather warnings guide, landing pages and in-app help links (W5)

A new "Weather warnings" guide page explains Met Office warnings in plain language (levels, the likelihood and impact matrix, "Updated" and "Raised from", where each warning appears, that a warning never changes a star rating, how fresh warnings are and that no warning is never an all-clear), with the required Met Office attribution. The At a Glance, Map, Coming up and Reading the forecast guides gain a Weather warnings section, and the guide hub, search, landing page, FAQ, acknowledgements and terms mention the warnings. Every warning source line in the app (the At a Glance lane, the window popup, the location sheet, the map card and chips, and Coming up) now ends with a `?` that opens the new guide. The stale threshold `photocast.warnings.poll-stale-minutes` now defaults to 10 minutes (was 30) to match the Met Office's timeliness bound. Dark until `photocast.warnings.enabled` is turned on; a "Flag-on checklist" is added to the warnings reference doc.
