### Fixed — 44px phone tap targets on the Operations screens

On a phone (640px and narrower) the Operations controls were 16 to 36px tall and easy to miss. The Lite limit
dialog (trigger, picks input, Save, Close), the temporary-password Copy and Close, the Areas tab (buttons,
inputs and member checkbox rows), the Aurora simulate modal, the Hot Topics simulation toggle and checkbox rows,
Rewind (buttons and date input), the Run Config tab buttons and extended-thinking toggle, and the Manage
section menu are now at least 44px tall. Phone-only classes, so tablet and desktop are unchanged.
