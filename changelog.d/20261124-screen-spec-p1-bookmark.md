### Changed — the bookmark mark, and a lens that only counts

Screen spec P1 (`docs/engineering/screen-spec.md` D1 and D7). A bookmarked place or area, and a watched date, are now marked with a lilac ribbon (`BookmarkMark`, an inline SVG) instead of a star, so the star means a rating and nothing else. The toggles read `Bookmark` / `Bookmarked` for a place or area and `Watch this date` / `Watching` for a date; the words carry the state, so `aria-pressed` is gone and the undo toast announces the change. The spoken suffix is `, bookmarked`, the toast says `Bookmarked X.`, and Ask's `rank_spots` schema says "bookmarked places". The lens readout is the count only at every width (`6 spots`, or `6 of 9 spots`): `formatLensReadout` and its tier, default and rating words are deleted. On a phone the lens is about 90 px (it was 130) and the screen's top padding is about 8 px (it was 37). The guides and the landing pricing list and FAQ say "Bookmark places and watch dates".
